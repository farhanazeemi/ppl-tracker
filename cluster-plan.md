## 3. Strict Internal Linking Quality Rules (AGENTS.md Compliant)

1. **Mandatory Workout Tracker Destination:**
   Every article discussing progressive overload or training routines must include a contextual, non-forced anchor link pointing directly to `https://ppltracker.web.id/tools/ppl-tracker`.
2. **Cluster Interlinking:**
   Articles within the same movement cluster link to at least 2 neighboring sibling articles.
3. **Descriptive Anchor Text:**
   Never use "click here", "read more", or generic phrases. Always use descriptive anchors like `[low-incline dumbbell press technique guide](/blog/low-incline-dumbbell-press-upper-chest-hypertrophy)`.
