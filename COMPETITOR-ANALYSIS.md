## 2. In-Depth Competitor Breakdown

### 1. RP Hypertrophy App (Renaissance Periodization)
- **Strengths:** High domain authority, proprietary volume landmarks (MEV, MAV, MRV), Dr. Mike Israetel's YouTube reach.
- **Weaknesses:** High monthly cost (\$25–35/month), desktop web app requires complex account signup, high onboarding barrier.
- **PPL Tracker Advantage:** Zero payment, instant local-first access, instant double-progression calculation directly in the browser.

### 2. Hevy & Strong Apps
- **Strengths:** Clean mobile native UX, strong App Store presence, social workout sharing.
- **Weaknesses:** Freemium lockouts (e.g., maximum 3 custom routines on free tier), weak desktop web usability, lack of scientific literature/journal integration.
- **PPL Tracker Advantage:** Unlimited custom routines and periodized splits on any device (desktop, tablet, mobile), zero paywall restrictions, embedded plate math.

### 3. Boostcamp
- **Strengths:** Broad community workout library (GZCLP, nSuns, PPL, 5/3/1).
- **Weaknesses:** Cluttered UI, slow web client, coaching paywalls.
- **PPL Tracker Advantage:** Minimalist, zero-lag interface, automated double progression algorithms without third-party upsells.

### 4. Muscle & Strength / Bodybuilding.com
- **Strengths:** High ranking for high-volume head keywords (e.g. "push pull legs routine", "6 day workout split").
- **Weaknesses:** Outdated static text articles with downloadable PDF printouts, invasive display ad banners, zero interactive workout tracking in the browser.
- **PPL Tracker Advantage:** Modern interactive web application directly embedded into the user's workout flow; fast, distraction-free environment.

## 3. Strategic Content Gaps & Opportunities

1. **"Busy Gym" Exercise Swaps**:
   - Competitors provide static tables; PPL Tracker provides an interactive, deterministic **Workout Substitution Finder** matching equipment to resistance profiles.
2. **Barbell Plate Math**:
   - Most apps lock plate calculators behind pro subscriptions or third-party ad-ridden apps. PPL Tracker provides real-time Olympic barbell loading calculation natively.
3. **Double Progression Method Mechanics**:
   - Millions of lifters search for "how to progressive overload" but find vague advice. PPL Tracker provides a programmatic rep-range ceiling engine.
