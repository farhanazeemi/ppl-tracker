1. Low Incline Priority

Keep your incline bench between 15° to 30°. Going steeper shifts tension away from the clavicular (upper) pecs onto the front delts.

2. Double Progression

Keep weight fixed until you hit the top of the rep range across all sets with clean form. Increase weight at the next session and build back up.

3. Stretch Under Load

Control the descent for 2–3 seconds and avoid bouncing at the bottom of incline presses, lateral raises, and RDLs.
